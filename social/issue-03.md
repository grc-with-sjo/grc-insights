## Community summary (optional: only if it adds value; no link-dumping)

The EU's AI Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) moved AI Act high-risk duties to 2027-12-02 for uses like hiring and credit scoring and to 2028-08-02 for AI inside regulated products, but 2026-12-02 still brings marking duties for older generative AI systems and new prohibitions. On the privacy side, Ireland's Data Protection Commission fined Google EUR 403M over location data, and Dutch press reports a EUR 824.99M fine against Uber over automated driver deactivations that we could not check against the regulator's own decision. In the UK, automated-decision rules and a 30-day complaint acknowledgement duty already apply. Research on general-purpose AI enforcement and on the week of 2026-10-04 was incomplete, so treat those as unknown rather than quiet.
