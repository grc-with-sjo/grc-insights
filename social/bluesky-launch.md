## Field Notes thread (≤ 300 chars each; link in post 1 so it gets a card)

1/ In May I wrote down the 7 compliance shifts I think every GRC team should be tracking in 2026. Quick tour 🧵

https://grc-with-sjo.github.io/grc-insights/issues/2026-05-field-notes/

2/ AI governance → from guidance to enforcement.
Third-party risk → a regulatory duty, not best practice (hello, DORA).
Privacy → regulators issuing fines, not warnings.

3/ Cyber → a leadership responsibility (NIST CSF 2.0's new Govern function).
Annual control testing → increasingly hard to defend against continuous monitoring.

4/ ESG → real momentum, then real headwinds (EU Omnibus, the SEC climate rule).
GRC tech → from tracking the work to doing it.

5/ Some of it has already moved: the EU pushed high-risk AI duties to Dec 2027, and Canada's privacy bill is now C-36. So I'm turning it into a running notebook. Issue 01, on Canada, is next.
