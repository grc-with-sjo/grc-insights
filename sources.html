Sources & References

All sources cited in the article are listed below, organized by trend. Each links directly to the primary regulatory body, standards organization, or official publication. No third-party summaries or paywalled content.

Note on currency: Regulatory timelines and rule status change frequently. Dates and implementation phases reflect the best available information as of May 2026. Readers in regulated industries should verify current status directly with the issuing body before making compliance decisions. Nothing in this article or these references constitutes legal or regulatory advice.
Trend 01 AI Governance

    European Parliament & Council Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — The EU Artificial Intelligence Act. In force August 2024; prohibited AI systems banned February 2025; GPAI obligations August 2025. ↗ eur-lex.europa.eu Live

    National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), January 2023. Sector-specific profiles in development. ↗ airc.nist.gov Live

    International Organization for Standardization ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Artificial Intelligence Management System. Published December 2023. ↗ iso.org Live

    Parliament of Canada Bill C-27: Digital Charter Implementation Act, 2022 (including CPPA and Artificial Intelligence and Data Act). Bill died on order paper when Parliament prorogued January 2025. ↗ parl.ca Verify Status

  • 5
    European Parliament & Council Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 — Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA). Applicable to EU financial entities from January 17, 2025. ↗ eur-lex.europa.eu Live
  • 6
    U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission Final Rule: Cybersecurity Risk Management, Strategy, Governance, and Incident Disclosure (Release No. 33-11216). Effective for large accelerated filers December 2023. ↗ sec.gov Live

Trend 06 ESG Reporting
  • 14
    European Commission Directive 2022/2464 — Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD). Wave 1 in effect; EU Omnibus package (February 2025) proposed significant delays and scope reductions for later waves — verify current implementation timeline. ↗ eur-lex.europa.eu Verify Timeline
  • 15
    IFRS Foundation IFRS S1 (General Requirements) and IFRS S2 (Climate-related Disclosures). Issued June 2023. Adopted or under consultation in multiple jurisdictions. ↗ ifrs.org Live
  • 16
    International Auditing and Assurance Standards Board (IAASB) ISSA 5000 — International Standard on Sustainability Assurance. Published 2024. Establishes the framework for independent assurance on sustainability disclosures. ↗ iaasb.org Live
  • 17
    U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission Final Rule: Enhancement and Standardization of Climate-Related Disclosures (Release No. 33-11275). Released March 2024; stayed April 2024 pending litigation. Verify current rule status — significant changes occurred under new SEC leadership from January 2025. ↗ sec.gov Verify Status
