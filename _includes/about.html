Surabhi Joshi
Governance, Risk & Compliance Strategist · Vancouver, BC

Surabhi has spent over a decade at the intersection of compliance, technology, and risk. Her career has tracked the evolution of the audit profession itself, from financial audit to IT audit to the questions nobody has clean answers to yet: how do you govern AI? How do you keep risk models human-centric when the systems are not?

She has built and operationalized security, privacy, and compliance programmes across complex, multi-product environments, spanning SOC 2, ISO 27001, SOX-ICFR, and global privacy frameworks (GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, FOIPPA), and is known for translating regulatory expectations into controls that engineering, product, and operations teams can actually own and sustain.

She is based in Vancouver, BC, and writes these field notes to think through problems in public.
