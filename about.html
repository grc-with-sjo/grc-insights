About

Surabhi Joshi

Governance, risk and compliance practitioner in Vancouver, BC. I write about how AI, privacy and security are changing risk and compliance, and how governance needs to keep up.

Who I am

I have spent over a decade at the intersection of compliance, technology and risk. My career has followed the evolution of the audit profession itself: from financial audit, to IT audit, to the questions nobody has clean answers to yet. How do you govern AI? How do you keep risk models human-centric when the systems are not?

Most of my work is translating regulatory expectations into controls that engineering, product and operations teams can actually own and sustain, across complex, multi-product environments.

What I work on

  • AI governance: how organisations inventory, assess and oversee the AI they build and buy.
  • Privacy: programmes that hold up under GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA and BC's FOIPPA, and the new laws following them.
  • Security and compliance programmes: SOC 2, ISO 27001 and SOX-ICFR, built to run continuously rather than once a year.
  • Third-party and vendor risk: knowing what your suppliers do with your data, and your customers' data.

What you'll find here

Field notes on the shifts that matter to GRC teams, each with my take and the actions I would take, plus the Regulation Radar, a living tracker of the AI, privacy and cybersecurity laws and standards I'm watching. Start with the latest issues.
