How it works

GRC Insights is my running notebook on how the AI world is evolving and how governance should adapt: privacy law, AI regulation, and what both mean for tech, product/SaaS, cloud, hardware, banking and retail.

Cadence. A six-part weekly catch-up series (October–November 2026), then one issue a month. The Regulation Radar is updated as laws move.

Method. Research and first drafts are AI-assisted. Every claim links to a source, and every issue is reviewed, edited and approved by me before it is published.
